Elect Your Battle Chief

October 2, 2026

Your mission deserves to be on Pat McAfee Show. Men watch Pat McAfee show. Your mission belongs on Pat McAfee Show. Where many men will hear. Who cares about what comes out of his mouth. Seriously.

Your Battle Chief is the person you listen to. Without question. Without thought.

Now, they may not be your Head Honcho. There is a difference behind your Head Honcho and your Battle Chief. Your Head Honcho can surely lead from behind front lines. As they are Head Honcho. Head Honcho can whisper secrets into ear of Battle Chief to cry for their nation. To cry for their people’s. For nation and peoples to be inspired. To run directly into storm. Crying. Crying for their people’s well being. Crying for the good fight that is worth fighting.

Am I also curious. Per chance? Who will I have the great pleasure of serving? I would like to know who I will serve as we storm Pat McAfee Show in the name of your mission.

It is a fun mystery. Who will be your Battle Chief?

Whose word will your people serve? Whose actions will your people follow? Who will you follow directly into the storm?
