Reiki

Energy work for those ready to come home to themselves.

Reiki is a Japanese form of energy healing. The word itself translates to "universal life energy." The practice works with the body's energetic field — clearing blockages, restoring flow, and creating the conditions for the body to heal itself.

It is gentle, non-invasive, and deeply restorative. A session involves the gentle placement of hands on or above the body, allowing energy to move where it is most needed. Many people experience warmth, a sense of deep relaxation, emotional release, or simply a feeling of being held.

Reiki does not diagnose or treat illness. It works beneath the surface — at the level where stress, old patterns, and stored emotion live in the body. It asks nothing of you except that you show up and receive.

"It removed blockages I wasn't aware of, allowing me to further embrace my authentic expression."

I came to Reiki not as a practitioner first, but as someone who needed it.

When I receive energy work, something shifts — what had been held releases, and what is true becomes easier to live from. That experience is what drew me into the practice and what I bring to every session.

I am a Level 2 practitioner, trained to work with the energetic body both in person and across distance. Level 2 attunement deepens the capacity to channel energy and introduces sacred symbols that strengthen and focus the work — including the ability to send healing across time and space.
