Sunset Frontier

My name is Samuel Puthoff. I am an artist, traveler, golf caddie, energy worker, and fire tender.

Before the world of ceremony, caddying, creativity, and cowboying, I was walking a path that wasn't mine. Caught in patterns of numbing and disconnection.

I know what it's like to chase the title and still feel unfulfilled. To believe numbing is the only way to find peace. To feel alone in a room full of people. To feel called to something more but not know how to step toward it.

Over the past four years — through mentors, wisdom keepers, family, friends, strangers, and the grace of God — I've come to learn the reward of walking your own path. The cleansing that comes through immersion in nature. The long term benefits of inner work. The power of men stepping into something hard together.

My work now is creating spaces where others can step out of routine, reconnect with themselves, and begin walking toward something that feels true.
